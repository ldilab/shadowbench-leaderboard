A benchmark for semantic alignment in Lean 4 autoformalization. SA-PASS checks whether generated code compiles and expresses the intended theorem.

Metric definitions
Compile rate
Share of tasks whose generated Lean code compiles standalone.
SA-PASS (Soft)
Mean forward and backward checker pass rate, averaged over all tasks. Non-compiling tasks score zero.
SA-PASS
Share of tasks that compile and pass every forward and backward checker.
